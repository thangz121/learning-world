# VIETNAMESE CHILD DATA GAP — WP-1.9.26

> **Tóm tắt (VI):** Phát biểu chính xác: "No publicly accessible corpus matching the required
> criteria was identified in the audited sources." Có dataset nghiên cứu không công khai. Khoảng
> trống gồm: tuổi 4–6, Việt-L1, tiếng Anh, nhãn phone-level, license thương mại — cả bốn cùng lúc.

## Gap statement

| requirement | status |
|---|---|
| publicly accessible | NO (research datasets only) |
| Vietnamese-L1 children | research datasets exist but not distributed |
| ages 4–6 | VietSpeech includes 2;0–8;10; Phạm 2019 covers 2;2–5;11; Lee 2024 covers 3–7 |
| English pronunciation targets | VietSpeech includes English (DEAP); others are Vietnamese |
| phone-level labels | none published |
| commercial license | none |

## Realistic acquisition routes

1. **University collaboration** — Charles Sturt University (VietSpeech; Prof. Sharynne McLeod /
   Ben Phạm), Hue University (Lee et al.), Hanoi/USTH groups. Requires a data-sharing agreement.
2. **Local supervised collection** — the protocol designed in this WP; 30–50 children, 4–6,
   consent + assent, target-phone balanced (final consonants and /r/ oversampled).
3. **Kindergarten/school partnership** — recruitment and ethics approval; the highest-effort,
   highest-relevance route.

## What is NOT acceptable

- Substituting adult Vietnamese speech for child data.
- Substituting Vietnamese speech for English pronunciation targets.
- Substituting Mandarin-L1 child data (speechocean762) for Vietnamese-L1 without stating the
  domain limitation.
