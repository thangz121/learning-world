# AI Speak / M-Speak — Technical Evidence

## Verified (E1)
- M-Speak is proprietary AI embedded in Monkey ABC/Stories games and AI Speak app.
- Flow: child hears prompt → repeats → system records → score + comments; stars (1–3) in Monkey ABC.
- Aspects advertised: Pronunciation, Intonation, Fluency; Word Stress per word ("Đúng/Sai").
- Child reporting: per-page and whole-story pronunciation aggregation (Monkey Stories).
- No public paper, patent, SDK, API, or open-source code found.

## Claims (E0 — contradictory granularity)
- App Store: "scoring down to each syllable"
- Monkey Junior product page: "feedback down to each phoneme"
- Both cannot be assumed true without product testing; not treated as verified.

## Privacy (E1)
- Policy lists personal data only (name/email/phone/Facebook); retention "until deletion request".
- **No statement found about voice recording storage, cloud processing, or training use.**
  For LWE child-audio constraints this is an open risk, not a verified practice.
