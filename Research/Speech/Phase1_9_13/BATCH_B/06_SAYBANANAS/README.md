# BATCH B · SYSTEM 06 — SayBananas (Say66)

STATUS: **research + product documentation audited** (E1/E2); app not testable here (mobile game,
clinician/parent setup).

Key findings:
- E2 effectiveness study (45 rural Australian children with SSD, ages 4;4–10;5): high-dose
  practice (target ~100 trials/session; median 44.7 achieved); significant gains on treated
  words; **dose–response** (sessions ↔ improvement).
- E2 (independent handout): **template matching** architecture — before play, users record a
  "correct" and an "incorrect" template per word; during play the child's recording is compared
  to the templates and classified → "Good job!" / "Not quite".
  - **"fewer than half of the recordings could be analyzed for accuracy due to audio quality
    issues"** — external confirmation of our assessability/audio-quality problem.
  - Automated analysis agreed with expert clinicians **more than parent feedback did**.
- E2 (UNH thesis): game design — stars are mandatory collectibles that trigger speech exercises
  during gameplay (not after); parent feedback options: correct / move on / try again; max one
  retry per word (avoid practicing errors); KR/KP feedback principles; parent training day.
- Current product (say66.com, E0/E1): AI feedback + metrics, automatic difficulty adaptation,
  progress reports for parents/clinicians.
