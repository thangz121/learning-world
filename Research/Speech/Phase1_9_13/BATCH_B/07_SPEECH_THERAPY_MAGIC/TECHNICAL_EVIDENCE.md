# SpeechTherapyMagic — Technical Evidence (E0/E1)

## Scoring (site description, verbatim fragments)
- "The child taps to record, says the word, and taps again. **Two listeners score the clip, a
  specialist engine we trained on children's speech and an industry-standard one**, and the
  result shows a score for the word plus a breakdown of every sound in it: **which one slipped,
  what it sounded like instead, and a tip for the next try**."
- "Every attempt is saved to the child's progress by sound."

## Product architecture
- Games + pronunciation scoring + deck creator (Magic Studio, 22 element types) + Story Builder +
  flashcards/word bank + printables + progress dashboard.
- SLP assigns practice; shares decks by 6-digit PIN; child plays without account; per-card
  results return to the creator.
- Adult-managed accounts; no ads; data minimization; no child signup.
