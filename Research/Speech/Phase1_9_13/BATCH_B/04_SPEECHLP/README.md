Key E1 findings (The Science of Speech Sound Selection):
- Developmental norms from **Crowe & McLeod 2020** (US English consonant acquisition):
  - 2y: /b, n, m, p, h, w, d/
  - 3y: /g, k, f, t, ŋ, j/
  - 4y: /v, dʒ, tʃ, s, z, l, ʃ/
  - 5y: /ɹ, ð, ʒ/
  - 6y: /θ/
- **Rule: a word's minimum developmental age = the latest-developing consonant it contains;
  multisyllabic words +1 year (capped at 6+).**
- Filters: position (initial/medial/final), initial & final blends, phonological **process
  filter** (stopping, fronting, gliding, cluster reduction, **Final Consonant Deletion (FCD)**,
  WSD, ICD, assimilation…).
- Age-relative difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard) from: late-sound load, mid-level sounds,
  syllable count, complexity flags. "Difficulty is relative to the child's developmental age."
- App: ages 3–9, 1000+ words (R/S/TH/L + all consonants + blends), voice-controlled games,
  **"Smart voice detection separates their phonetic speech from the game's own music and sounds,
  so every score reflects a real attempt"** (attempt detection), on-device audio, HIPAA/COPPA,
  sound-by-sound tracking; screener = "useful first signal, not a diagnosis".
