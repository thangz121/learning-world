# VIETNAMESE-L1 CHILD SPEECH GAP — WP-1.9.25 Phase 7

> **Tóm tắt (VI):** Không tìm thấy corpus tiếng Việt trẻ em nào **truy cập công khai** đáp ứng tiêu chí
> (10–30 h, 30–50+ người nói, nhãn phone-level, tuổi 4–6, license thương mại). Có bằng chứng về các
> **dataset nghiên cứu không phát hành công khai** (VietSpeech Multilingual Children — ĐH Charles

> đều là người lớn. Kết luận chính xác: "No publicly accessible corpus matching the required criteria
> was identified in the audited sources."

## 1. Search scope

Queries covered English and Vietnamese terminology; sources inspected include the Wikipedia list of
children's speech corpora, the CSU VietSpeech project pages, the Cambridge J. Child Language article
on Vietnamese children's stops, Zenodo/OpenSLR/HuggingFace listings, and Vietnamese ASR corpus
surveys (VIVOS, Bud500, VietSpeech social voice, VinBigData-VLSP2020-100h, ViMD, FOSD, LSVSC,
VietSpeech social voice). No corpus was downloaded.

## 3. Precise conclusion

- **No publicly accessible Vietnamese-L1 child speech corpus with phone-level labels was identified
  in the audited sources.**
- Vietnamese-L1 child speech data appears to exist only as **research datasets held by institutions**
  (VietSpeech; Lee et al. 2024). A B2 path using Vietnamese-L1 children would require a research
  collaboration/data-sharing agreement, not a download.
- Do not claim "no Vietnamese child corpus exists": the evidence supports "none publicly accessible
  matching the required criteria".

## 4. Consequence for B2

A Vietnamese-L1-domain B2 is not executable from public data today. The practical options remain
English child speech (Options A/B/D/E in `B2_DATA_ACQUISITION_OPTIONS.md`) with an explicit
domain-gap limitation, or a partnership route that is outside this research gate.
